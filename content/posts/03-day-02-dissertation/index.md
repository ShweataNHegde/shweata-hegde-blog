On the other hand, I have spent an hour on Instagram watching videos about this new show called _Bigg Boss Agniparikshe__. The idea is to audition 'common people' to become contestants in the reality show _Bigg Boss_. There are 4 judges, each of them ask those auditioning to perform humiliating tasks, like applying cow dung on the face, creating rifts between twin sisters, asking to be cringy, getting someone to tear (duplicate) Guinness World record certificate, etc. It's just miserable. How have we become a society where we are okay with humiliation as a form of entertainment? 

There are other ways to test endurance, determination, and personality, which doesn't involve loss of human dignity. Full stop. Not to pedestalise the west, but I'm sure this show wouldn't have aired here at all.

I don't know what delusional bubble these media creators live in. The reality shows are starting to lack reality. Maybe ask people to champion a cause: climate change, cleanliness drive, education, etc. Or, ask someone to demonstrate their skills, art, etc. 

I'm glad some creators are speaking up against the show. Popular media needs a reset in India. Enshittification, is not just an internet phenomenon, it's also a media one. Sigh.
