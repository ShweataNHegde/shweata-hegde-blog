If this blog site was a reflection of my life, my absence might signal a stand still. Yet, my days have been vibrant than ever, full of new discoveries and learning. Except, I'm very tired. Very tired physically. 

For many years now, I considered myself 'weak'. Something as simple as going out to a book store would tire me. I thought it was because I didn't eat well, or that I didn't exercise enough, etc. But then, my tiredness got worse over the years. Getting out of bed on some days became a challenge. I'd wake up with soreness and pain all over, pain that did not match the moderate, calibrated, physical exertion from the previous day. Iron levels, normal. Vitamin D, within the range. B12 fine. None of the usual suspects gave answers. It was clear something else was going on. And that's what I've spent a lot of my time here in London discovering about myself with my GPs and informative friends.

I very likely have a chronic genetic condition which might be causing all these symptoms that have taken over some aspects of my life. Many have asked how I have taken this news. When I first learnt about it, it didn't come as shock at all. I'd been desperate for answers and it was almost calming to finally have one. The answer allowed gave myself a reason to be kind to myself.

What I'm unable to do is document. A portion of the time and energy I used to have to go the extra mile to write about my day is taken away by sheer exhaustion. The other day, I was reading my Young India Fellowship weekly diary. Although it was physically and emotionally exhausting to do the Fellowship; I just about managed to keep up.

But, I'm grateful that I still get to enjoy life. Go out at my own pace, mostly to galleries, museums and parks. Meet friends from time to time (I'll be off to a Diwali party now), spend time with our cats, read when I can, work on projects that brings me joy. I'm still in London, enjoying the last few weeks before I fly back to India...
