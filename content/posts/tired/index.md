If this blog was a reflection of my life, my absence might signal a stand still. Yet, my days have been vibrant, full of new discoveries and learning, except that I'm very tired. Very tired physically.

For many years now, I considered myself 'weak'. Something as simple as going out to a book store would tire me. I thought it was all because I didn't eat well, exercise enough, etc. But then, my tiredness got worse over the years. Getting out of bed on some days was a challenge. I'd have soreness and pain all over, that did not match the moderate (callibreated almost) physical exertion from the previous day. While they are all true, there's more to this. And that's what I've spent a lot of my time here in London discovering with my GPs and informative friends.

That discovery has been a slow revelation. I very likely have a chronic genetic condition which might be causing all these symptoms that have taken over some aspects of my life.

Now, this post isn't me saying that I've been stuck to bed not being able to do much. Luckily, I haven't reached that point. I still enjoy life. Go out at my own pace, mostly to galleries, museums and parks. Meet friends from time to time (I'll be off to a Diwali party now).

What I'm unable to do is document. A portion of the time and energy I used to have to go the extra mile to write about my day is taken away by just exhaustion. The other day, I was reading my YIF weekly diary. Although it was physically and emotionally exhausting to do the Fellowship; I don't know, I don't know how I found the time to keep up the streak.
