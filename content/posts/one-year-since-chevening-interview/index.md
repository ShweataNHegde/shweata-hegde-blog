I still remember 9th April from 2025. Many years of dreaming, a month of regime-like preparation, it all came down to a half an hour interview. I remember being extremely anxious, nervous, and sweaty. Little did I know that this unbearably hot and sunny day of Sonipat was going to change my life forever.

Now, a year later, I am in London. Living by myself. I have my own room with own washroom. I live with the most amazing people. I have good friends. I am taking care of my health more responsibly. I have two loving cats. I will soon be going on a solo-trip. I study what I love: science communication. I am setting up a student society for science communication. I go around London seeing places. Isn't this living the dream?

Of course, this is not gloss over the challenges. The world order seems to be deteriorating just when I think it cannot get worse. Doing therapy, being uncomfortable is difficult. I'm already dealing anticipatory anxiety of heading back home in 6 months. Funnily, on the day of my Chevening interview last year, I also interviewed for Science Gallery Bengaluru and had recieved an offer from them. Hopefully, they will be kind enough to take me in once I go back.

I am grateful for life. 9th April is going to be a special day for the rest of my life. The day that restored faith in myself, fixed self-doubts, proved that all it takes is one opportunity to work out. That day, I had two.
